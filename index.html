Build details

How we build it

The plan so far, with ideas from the parent community. Each piece is a job someone can take.

Backdrop
A canvas drop cloth, 12 by 20 ft or bigger, painted by Ben Wilson: a mountain top under dramatic clouds. It stands on its own frame in front of the black curtain. Nothing hangs from the stage. Drop cloth from a paint or hardware store. Paint and spray paint.
The summit
The throne sits on our three pallets, stacked, screwed together and decked with plywood. Cardboard shapes the rock, burlap goes over it, paint makes it stone. Three solid steps up the front so kids can climb on. Pallets in hand. Plywood, burlap and screws from EcoSet or a hardware store.
Mountain peaks
Tall peaks to each side: cardboard volumes skinned in burlap, sackcloth or paper mache, then painted and spray painted as rock. Smaller flat peaks near the camera, out of focus, to frame the shot. Appliance and furniture boxes. Burlap or sackcloth. Newspaper and glue.
Clouds
Pillow stuffing, lots of it. Chicken wire or draped sheets give the shape. The stuffing goes over the top. Clouds around the foot of the summit and along the front of the stage, fog behind. Old pillows and comforters from families, Buy Nothing, thrift. Chicken wire from a hardware store.
Columns
At least two, ideally four, either side of the summit. Cardboard tubes or rolled cardboard on a wood base, painted as marble. Maybe two small ones right by the camera, for depth. Carpet and concrete-form tubes. EcoSet. Appliance-store cardboard.
Throne and urns
A high-back chair sprayed gold, screwed to the summit. Two big urns with olive trees either side of the steps: planters painted stone, branches sprayed grey-green. Also worth building: a gold eagle on a column, torches with LED flame, laurel garlands on the columns. Thrift and Buy Nothing for the chair and planters. Fake branches, gold spray.
Hand props
Things to grab and pose with. The bolt, the trident, the golden apple, laurel wreaths, a giant mallet, a scythe. More if they come easy: Medusa's head, a lyre, a bow, a gold shield, a winged helmet, a golden fleece. Cardboard, foam board, pool noodles, dowels. Gold and silver spray. Dollar Tree ivy.
